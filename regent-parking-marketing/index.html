The Problem

Visitor parking is usually held together by habit, not a system.

A binder at the concierge desk. A text message to the super. No record of who parked where, or for how long — until someone's car gets towed and the board needs an answer nobody has.

01

Residents register in seconds

One link, no app to install. A resident enters their unit, their code, and a plate — registration takes under a minute, and extensions are built in so a longer visit doesn't mean starting over.

02

Built for the garage, not a desk

Concierge and security get a large-text, mobile-first view of every valid plate — exempt vehicles clearly flagged — so a walk-through takes a glance, not a login and a squint at a spreadsheet.

03

An answer, whenever the board asks

Every registration — active or expired — is searchable by plate or by unit for three years. When a question comes up about who parked where and when, there's a real answer, not a guess.

Built for Boards, Not Just IT

Fair limits, set by your board — enforced automatically.

Every unit gets a monthly visitor-pass allowance and a cap on how many days any single plate can stay registered — both set by your board, not fixed by us. No manual tracking, no arguments at the desk — whatever limits you choose, the system enforces them the same way for every resident.

  • Monthly visitor passes per unit — you set the number, resets automatically
  • A day-cap per plate, so one "visitor" can't quietly become a permanent tenant
  • Starts at 10 passes and 7 days by default, changeable anytime from your own admin portal
  • Vehicle exemptions for contractors and vendors, with a start and end date
  • Multiple buildings or towers under one account, managed independently
Privacy, By Design

We collect less than you'd think — on purpose.

Regent Parking doesn't ask residents for a phone number, and never exposes a unit's parking code to a browser — it's checked server-side and only ever returns yes or no. Registration history is kept for three years, then automatically and permanently deleted, so nothing is retained longer than it needs to be.
