What Residents See

Registering a visitor takes less than a minute.

STEP 1

Enter the unit number and code

Every unit has its own private code, set by building management — never a parking spot number or anything a stranger could guess.

STEP 2

Add the visitor's plate (and a spot, if used)

If the building uses numbered visitor spots, residents pick one here too. Buildings that don't use that feature never see this step at all.

STEP 3

Submit — protected automatically

An invisible, score-based security check runs in the background. No puzzle, no checkbox — nothing for the resident to solve.

STEP 4

Confirmed, with an expiry time

The resident sees their expiry and remaining monthly passes immediately, and can extend later without starting over.

What Your Concierge or Security Team Sees

A lot walk-through takes a glance, not a login and a spreadsheet.

STEP 1

Today's valid plates, already loaded

Every currently-registered vehicle for the building, in large text built for a phone held in one hand.

STEP 2

Tap each match while walking the lot

A quick tap checks a plate off the list — no typing, no searching through a binder.

STEP 3

Whatever's left needs attention

Anything still unchecked at the end of the walk-through is exactly what's unregistered — flag it in one tap.

STEP 4

Exempt vehicles are called out automatically

Contractors and vendors with an approved exemption are marked clearly, so staff never have to second-guess a familiar vehicle.

What Your Board Controls

Every rule is yours to set — and change anytime.

STEP 1

Pick a building

Multiple towers or buildings live under one account, each configured independently.

STEP 2

Turn on numbered visitor spots (optional)

Most buildings don't need this — but if yours assigns specific spots to visitors, it's one toggle away.

STEP 3

Set the range

V1 through V20, or whatever your building actually has. Residents only ever see a dropdown of real, available spots.

STEP 4

It shows up everywhere else, automatically

The same spot appears on the resident's confirmation and in the enforcement view — no second system to keep in sync.
