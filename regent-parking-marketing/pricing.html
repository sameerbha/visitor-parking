Frequently asked

Does the price change with more towers or buildings?

No. If your condo corporation has more than one tower or building, the flat $109/month rate covers all of them under one account — it does not multiply per address.

Is there a cost per unit or per resident?

No. Pricing is per building corporation, not per door. A 400-unit building and a 40-unit building pay the same rate.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee. Getting a building running means loading in unit numbers and issuing parking codes — something we handle as part of onboarding.

What's not included at this price?

Custom feature requests specific to one building are scoped and quoted separately. Everything described on this page — the resident, staff, and admin tools as they exist today — is included for every customer at the same flat rate.

How do we get started?

Reach out on the contact page and we'll walk through your building's setup — unit list, towers, and current process — before anything goes live.
