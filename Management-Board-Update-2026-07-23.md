Subject: Visitor Parking App — Summary of Recent Improvements

Dear Board and Management,

Over the past several weeks the visitor parking app has gone through a significant round of improvements — to the resident experience, the tools concierge and security use day to day, data privacy, and reporting. Summary below.

**Registration is now one tap, not two.** Residents used to land on a menu and click through to the registration form. The form now loads immediately — same link, one fewer step between opening the site and getting a visitor's car registered.

**Concierge and security have a purpose-built mobile tool.** Checking plates in the garage previously meant working from a desk-oriented table not really designed for a phone. There's now a "Patrol View": a clean, large-text list of every valid plate, with exempt vehicles clearly flagged so staff know at a glance not to ticket them. When a car isn't on the list, staff can log the plate, spot number, and a quick description right from their phone — no paper notebook — and copy the full list with one tap when they're back at the desk calling it in to the city.

**We stopped collecting phone numbers.** The app no longer asks residents for a phone number at all, and any numbers previously stored have been removed from the database. It's one less piece of personal information being held without a clear ongoing need for it.

**New reporting for staff.** Three things are now available in the Admin portal: a unit's remaining monthly visitor permits, the full registration history for any license plate, and the full registration history for any unit — active and expired alike, searchable on demand. Alongside this, we set a deliberate retention period: registration history is kept for 3 years and then automatically and permanently deleted, rather than being retained indefinitely by default.

**A number of reliability and usability fixes**, including a bug that could intermittently show staff the wrong screen, and a mobile redesign pass — larger buttons, bigger text, easier-to-use search — tested against the iPhone SE through 14 and Galaxy S22/S23 range, since that's what staff actually use in the garage.

Happy to walk through any of this live, or answer questions in more detail.
