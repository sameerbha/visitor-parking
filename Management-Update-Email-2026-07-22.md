Subject: Visitor Parking App — Recent Updates

Hi all,

Quick update on the visitor parking app — a round of improvements went live today aimed at making things easier for residents, faster for concierge/security on the ground, and tighter on privacy. Highlights below.

**Registration is now one tap, not two.** Residents used to land on a menu and have to click through to the registration form. The form is now the first thing that loads — same link, one fewer step between "open the site" and "car is registered." Staff tools (Enforcement, Admin) still have their own dedicated access point, just tucked out of the resident's way.

**Concierge and security now have a purpose-built tool for walking the garage.** Previously, checking plates meant a desk-oriented table not really designed for a phone. There's now a dedicated "Patrol View": a clean, large-text list of every valid plate, plus exempt vehicles clearly flagged so staff know at a glance not to ticket them. When they find a car that isn't on the list, they can log the plate, spot number, and a quick description right from their phone — no more paper notebooks — and copy the whole list with one tap when they're back at the desk calling it in to the city.

**We stopped collecting phone numbers.** In line with the data-minimization principle from the recent security review, the app no longer asks residents for a phone number at all, and any previously stored numbers are being removed from the database. One less piece of personal information we're holding without a clear ongoing need for it.

**Several reliability and usability fixes**, including a bug where the enforcement/admin pages could intermittently show the wrong screen, and sizing/layout improvements so the app is genuinely comfortable to use one-handed on a phone (this was tested against the iPhone SE/12/13/14 and Galaxy S22/S23 size range).

Happy to walk through any of this in more detail or take a live look together.
