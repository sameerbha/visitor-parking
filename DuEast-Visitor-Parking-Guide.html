How to Register a Visitor
1

Open the registration page

On your phone or computer, go to dueastparking.netlify.app. The registration form opens right away — no extra taps needed.

2

Enter your unit number

Type your unit number exactly as it appears on your lease — for example W403 or E805.

3

Enter your unit code

Enter the unique parking code assigned to your unit by building management. It's case-insensitive. Don't have a code? Contact the concierge.

4

Enter your visitor's license plate

Type the plate number exactly as it appears on the vehicle (e.g. ABCD 123). Double-check — it must match for enforcement staff.

5

Complete the security check

Answer the simple math question on screen to confirm you're not a bot.

6

Submit

Tap Register Visitor. A confirmation screen will show the expiry time and your remaining monthly passes.

⏱

Extending an existing registration

If your visitor's plate is already active, the system will offer to extend it by 24 hours instead of creating a duplicate. Just confirm the extension on screen — no need to re-enter your details.

Monthly Limits
10
passes / month
Each unit may register up to 10 visitor passes per calendar month.
7
days / plate
A single plate may be registered on no more than 7 distinct days per month.
24
hour validity
Each registration is valid for 24 hours from the time of submission.
Tips
  • 📱 The registration page is mobile-friendly — works on any phone or browser, no app needed.
  • ⏰ Register before your visitor arrives to avoid any delays at the entrance.
  • 📊 Your remaining monthly passes are shown on the confirmation screen after each registration.
  • 🔒 Your unit code is confidential — do not share it with anyone outside your household.
  • 🔄 Limits reset on the 1st of every month.
💬

Need help?

If you don't have a unit code, your registration is being blocked, or you have any questions, please contact the concierge desk or building management.
