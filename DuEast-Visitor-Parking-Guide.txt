HOW TO REGISTER A VISITOR
--------------------------

Step 1 — Open the registration page
  Go to dueastparking.netlify.app on your phone or computer.
  The registration form opens right away — no extra taps needed.

Step 2 — Enter your unit number
  Type your unit number exactly as it appears on your lease
  (e.g. W403 or E805).

Step 3 — Enter your unit code
  Enter the unique parking code assigned to your unit by
  building management. It is case-insensitive.
  Don't have a code? Contact the concierge.

Step 4 — Enter your visitor's license plate
  Type the plate number exactly as it appears on the vehicle
  (e.g. ABCD 123). Double-check — it must match exactly for
  enforcement staff.

Step 5 — Complete the security check
  Answer the simple math question on screen.

Step 6 — Submit
  Tap "Register Visitor". A confirmation screen will show the
  expiry time and your remaining monthly passes.


EXTENDING AN EXISTING REGISTRATION
------------------------------------
If your visitor's plate is already active, the system will
offer to extend it by 24 hours instead of creating a duplicate.
Just confirm the extension on screen — no need to re-enter
your details.


MONTHLY LIMITS
--------------
  10  passes per unit per calendar month
   7  days a single plate can be registered per month
  24  hours each registration is valid for

Limits reset on the 1st of every month.


TIPS
----
  - The registration page works on any phone or browser.
    No app needed.

  - Register BEFORE your visitor arrives to avoid delays
    at the entrance.

  - Your remaining monthly passes are shown on the
    confirmation screen after each registration.

  - Your unit code is confidential. Do not share it with
    anyone outside your household.


NEED HELP?
----------
If you don't have a unit code, your registration is being
blocked, or you have any questions, please contact the
concierge desk or building management.
